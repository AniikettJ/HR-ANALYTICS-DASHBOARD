# 👥 HR Analytics Dashboard

> **Employee Attrition & Workforce Intelligence | SQL | Power BI | DAX | Power Query**

An interactive HR analytics project built to transform employee data into actionable insights around **attrition, workforce composition, job roles, compensation, demographics, and employee experience**.

## 🎯 Business Objective

Employee attrition affects hiring costs, productivity, team stability, and workforce planning. This project answers questions such as:

- What is the overall employee attrition rate?
- Which departments and job roles have the highest attrition?
- How does attrition vary by gender and age group?
- Is compensation associated with employee turnover?
- How does experience influence attrition?

## 🧮 SQL Analysis

SQL was used for data validation, cleaning, aggregation, and business analysis, including:

- Record and NULL-value validation
- Data-type standardization
- Total employee and attrition calculations
- Attrition-rate calculation
- Department-wise and gender-wise workforce analysis
- Job-role attrition analysis
- Salary comparison between attrition and non-attrition employees
- Experience-based attrition analysis

## 💡 Key Insights

The analysis helps identify **high-attrition departments and job roles**, examine compensation and demographic patterns, and understand how employee experience relates to turnover.

The final dashboard converts these findings into an interactive **HR decision-support tool** for workforce and retention analysis.
